Introduction
Every square matrix can be associated with a number called its determinant.
Determinants occur in many mathematical topics. The coefficients in system of linear
equations are often represented by matrices and the determinant can be used to solve
systems of linear equations, if the coefficient matrix is an invertible matrix.
In this unit, determinant of a matrix; basic properties of determinant will be discussed
and some applications of determinants in solving systems of linear equations will
also be given.
