Introduction
Systems of linear equations occur in many areas such as in Geometry, Engineering,
Business, Economics, Biology, etc. Solving such systems of linear equations is one
of the most important applications of mathematics to other fields.
In Unit 3 of Grade 9, you have learned how to solve systems of linear equations in
two variables. In this unit, you will learn how to solve systems of linear equations in
a more systematic way.
