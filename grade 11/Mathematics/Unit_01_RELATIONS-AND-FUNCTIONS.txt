Introduction
In your daily life, you come across many patterns that characterize relationships
between individuals such as brother and sister, father and daughter or son, teacher
and student, etc. In mathematics also you come across many relationships such as a
line l is perpendicular to line m; a number a is less than a number b; a number C is a
factor of a number d, etc.
In this unit, the concepts about relations and functions that you have learned in Unit 1
of Grade 10 will be revised and some special types of functions such as power
functions, absolute value functions, signum functions and the greatest integer
functions and their properties will be discussed. Furthermore, some applications of
relations and functions will also be considered.
